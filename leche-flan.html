Leche Flan

Smooth, creamy caramel custard made for sharing.

Description

Leche flan is a rich and creamy Filipino custard made with egg yolks and milk, topped with sweet caramel. It is a classic dessert at Christmas, birthdays and fiestas.

Recipe information

Quick facts for Leche Flan
Preparation timeCooking timeServingsDifficulty
20 minutes (plus 4 hours chilling)50 minutes6 to 8Medium

Ingredients

  • 10 egg yolks
  • 1 can (14 oz) condensed milk
  • 1 can (12 oz) evaporated milk
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 3/4 cup white sugar (for the caramel)
  • Aluminum foil
  • 1 llanera (oval mold) or round baking dish

Instructions

  1. Melt the sugar in the mold over low heat until golden brown. Tilt to coat the bottom and let it harden.
  2. Gently mix the egg yolks, condensed milk, evaporated milk and vanilla without making bubbles.
  3. Strain the mixture twice, then pour into the caramel-coated mold and cover tightly with foil.
  4. Steam on low heat for 40 to 50 minutes until set.
  5. Cool, then chill at least 4 hours. Run a knife around the edge, flip onto a plate and serve.
