Chicken Adobo

Chicken simmered in soy sauce, vinegar and garlic.

Description

Adobo is often called the unofficial national dish of the Philippines. Chicken is simmered in soy sauce, vinegar, garlic, bay leaves and peppercorns until tender, giving a salty, tangy and savory sauce that goes perfectly with steamed rice.

Recipe information

Quick facts for Chicken Adobo
Preparation timeCooking timeServingsDifficulty

Ingredients

  • 1 kg chicken thighs or drumsticks
  • 1/2 cup soy sauce
  • 1/2 cup white or cane vinegar
  • 1 head garlic, peeled and crushed
  • 3 dried bay leaves
  • 1 teaspoon whole black peppercorns
  • 1 cup water
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil
  • 1 tablespoon brown sugar (optional)

Instructions

  1. Marinate the chicken in the soy sauce and half of the garlic for 30 minutes.
  2. Heat the oil and sauté the remaining garlic until lightly golden.
  3. Add the chicken and brown each side for about 3 minutes.
  4. Pour in the marinade, vinegar and water. Add the bay leaves and peppercorns.
  5. Bring to a boil without stirring for 2 minutes, then simmer covered for 30 minutes.
  6. Simmer uncovered for 5 to 10 minutes until the sauce thickens. Serve with steamed rice.
