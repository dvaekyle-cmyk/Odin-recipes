Filipino recipe

Sinigang na Baboy

A sour, comforting pork soup with fresh vegetables.

Description

Sinigang is a comforting Filipino soup known for its sour broth, traditionally made with tamarind. Tender pork and fresh vegetables are cooked together, making it a favorite on rainy days.

Recipe information

Quick facts for Sinigang na Baboy
Preparation timeCooking timeServingsDifficulty
15 minutes1 hour 15 minutes4 to 6Medium

Ingredients

  • 1 kg pork belly or ribs, cut into pieces
  • 8 cups water
  • 1 large tomato, quartered
  • 1 medium onion, quartered
  • 1 pack (40 g) sinigang mix, or about 10 fresh tamarind fruits
  • 2 long green chili peppers
  • 1 small white radish, sliced
  • 1 eggplant, sliced
  • 8 string beans, cut into 2-inch pieces
  • 1 bunch water spinach (kangkong)
  • Fish sauce and salt to taste

Instructions

  1. Boil the water and add the pork, onion and tomato.
  2. Simmer covered for 45 to 60 minutes until the pork is tender. Skim off any scum.
  3. Stir in the sinigang mix (or the boiled, mashed tamarind).
  4. Add the radish and cook 5 minutes, then add the eggplant, string beans and chili for 5 more minutes.
  5. Season with fish sauce and salt, add the kangkong and turn off the heat. Serve hot with rice.
